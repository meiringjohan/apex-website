FAQ

Is Home really free?

Yes. No cost, no time limit. Race at home on one track with 2 to 4 lanes. Optional yearly add-ons unlock Championship, Endurance and Stream Overlay.

What’s the difference between Home and Club?

Club adds 2–8 lanes, phone check-in, marshals, timed practice, events/calendar, two race screens (six with Screen Studio), full car catalogue import, and the Club add-ons. Home is capped at 6 drivers racing at once (12 in an Endurance race).

Do I need new hardware?

No. APEX works with the APEX lap counter, Trackmate, or an Arduino running Race Coordinator’s program — and corrects all of them.

Can I keep my Race Coordinator / TrackMate history?

Yes — import database or exports (drivers, races, results, rotations and the rest).

Is there a Club trial?

Yes — 30 days of Club base. Add-ons are not part of the trial.

How do licences work?

Per computer and per track. Checked offline. Add-ons have a last day and a grace period. Moving computer: issue a replacement key that voids the old serial.

What about languages?

The app speaks English, Spanish and Italian. This website stays English-only.

When does it launch?

February 2027. Club testing from mid-December 2026. Keep me posted via the home page.

Does a busy PC spoil the times?

No. Laps are timed on the lap counter; the computer only receives them. See How timing works.

Stream Overlay vs Broadcast Studio?

Home buys Stream Overlay for the tower and results board. Club includes that module. Broadcast Studio adds the directed broadcast tools (zones, director, sponsors, phone remote).
