Screens & streaming

How the hall and the stream look — race screens, phones, and overlays.

Race screens

  • ✓Live board: laps, last and best, gap, laps led, average, records, on deck, marshals
  • ✓Block or List layouts; Club second screen; Screen Studio up to six
  • ✓Between races: calendar, club image, QR codes
  • ✓Voice call-outs offline (male/female), never a driver’s name
  • ✓Any number of browsers can show the race screen at once
  • ✓A library of ready-made formats, plus your own

Phones

  • ✓Drivers scan the race-screen QR: check-in and personal results — no app, no login
  • ✓Race Control on phone: Track call, resume, keep screen on
  • ✓Broadcast remote with Broadcast Studio

Streaming

  • ✓Home Stream Overlay / Club base: TV tower + results board into OBS and friends
  • ✓Broadcast Studio: director, zones, sponsors, phone remote
