Home · Phone

Phone

Drivers, marshals and race directors open APEX in the phone browser — no app to install. The phone page lists what you can do right now and follows race control by itself.

Who it’s for. Drivers following a race or downloading results; race directors running Race Control from a phone; Club check-in and next-heat cards; Endurance Plus teams; Broadcast Studio directors; Championship Plus scrutineers.

At a glance

  • Phone page
  • Live results
  • Race screen
  • Race Control
  • Results
  • Check-in
  • Next heat
  • Team phone
  • Broadcast remote
  • Scrutineer
HomeClub

Phone page (hub)

Scan the QR on the race screen (or a printed QR sheet) and the phone page opens. It needs no login for results and the race screen. Options appear as race control opens them.

  • ✓Live results while a race runs
  • ✓Results after a race finishes
  • ✓Check-in or sign-in at Club meetings
  • ✓Team phone in an endurance race
  • ✓Stream director with Broadcast Studio
  • ✓Log in for Race Control
HomeClub

Live results

While a race is on, Live results shows the race, heat and time to go, then the running order. It updates by itself.

  • ✓Position, driver, laps, best lap
  • ✓Gap to the leader
  • ✓Updates every few seconds
HomeClub

Race screen on a phone

The same race screen as the TV, sized for the phone.

  • ✓Lap records, heat and clock, start lights
  • ✓On deck
  • ✓Each lane’s driver box: laps, last and best, gap, laps led
HomeClub

Race Control on a phone

Log in with your APEX username and password to run Race Control from the phone browser.

  • ✓Track call and clock
  • ✓Main button: start / resume / confirm
  • ✓Autopilot and Keep screen on
  • ✓Restart heat and End race
  • ✓Running order
HomeClub

Results PDFs

After a race, download everyone’s results PDF, or pick your name for your own — one link for every driver, no login.

  • ✓Race results PDF for everyone
  • ✓My results: laps, times, reaction times, graphs
  • ✓Club: class-rules PDFs and provisional/final while scrutineering runs
Club

Club check-in

While race control has check-in open, drivers pick their name, tick today’s classes (and car when asked), and check in. The phone remembers you for next time.

  • ✓Pick name and classes
  • ✓Car when the class asks for it
  • ✓Checked-in card with check out
Club

Next heat & penalties

Your driver card shows the next heat, lane and colour, and when to go. When the race allows penalty protests, My penalties lists what race control gave you.

  • ✓Next heat, lane and colour
  • ✓When to go (now / next / heats away)
  • ✓Protest or withdraw within the app’s window
Endurance Plus

Team phone

In an endurance race with team phones, each team follows the race and can send requests for race control to confirm.

  • ✓Position, gaps, who is driving
  • ✓Stint and next driver / lane
  • ✓Drive time and obligations
  • ✓Captain or PIN member can request changes; cancel a waiting request
Broadcast Studio

Broadcast remote

Runs the Stream director from a phone. A Broadcast director login opens the remote and nothing else; race coordinators and administrators can open it too.

  • ✓Auto / Assist / Manual
  • ✓ON AIR and take buttons
  • ✓Hold all, Back to auto, Clean feed
Championship Plus

Scrutineering

Open a scrutineer link (or log in as a scrutineer), pick a car and work through its checklist. You never see your own car in the list.

  • ✓Tick items or type measured values
  • ✓Limits show next to each item
  • ✓Add a photo where it helps
