Championship Plus

Club · / year (see Pricing for multi-year)

Everything in Championship, built for a club night calendar: schedule rounds weeks ahead, seed from saved lists, scrutineer with a phone link, and export results.

Who it’s for. Clubs that run a full season with seeding, dropped rounds, ahead-of-time scheduling and export.

Features

  • ✓Full seasons with dropped rounds and seeding (including saved seeding lists)
  • ✓Points, total laps or average-lap schemes; visiting-winner bonus; club championship
  • ✓Scheduling ahead — races and rounds saved as Ready until they start
  • ✓Scrutineering with phone link for a named scrutineer; decision log
  • ✓Excel / CSV / tab / plain text / JSON export of one race or a date range

Works with multi-day meetings. Carry-over rules between sessions live in Event Manager Plus.
