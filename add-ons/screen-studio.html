Screen Studio

Club · / year (see Pricing for multi-year)

Club base already includes two race screens. Screen Studio adds Screens 3 to 6, anywhere on the network, with the layout builder that advances with the race.

Who it’s for. Clubs that want more than two live screens — pit wall, foyer TV, commentary desk.

Features

  • ✓Add screens on this computer’s outputs or other machines on the LAN
  • ✓TV link / QR to open a screen on a TV
  • ✓Each screen has its own layout and between-heats cards
  • ✓Layout builder with live widget previews
  • ✓Heat widgets: Next drivers, Next marshals, Current marshals; Gap chart
  • ✓Race-type gates so a layout can’t be saved broken
