Endurance

Home · / year (see Pricing for multi-year)

One long race — by time or by laps — with every car on every lane in turn. Teams or single drivers, lane-change windows, pits, and a race that survives a power cut.

Who it’s for. Home teams or solo drivers who want a long race with stints, pits and lane changes.

Features

  • ✓Enduro by time or by laps; finish modes including run-to-the-line and stop-at-time
  • ✓Teams: fixed, drawn on the day, or paired by pace
  • ✓Lane changes with even time split across segments
  • ✓Pit in / pit out on Race Control; shortest-stop flag
  • ✓Track call with race time stopped until green; resume with start lights
  • ✓Optional qualifying before the enduro
  • ✓Standing or rolling start
  • ✓Home limit: up to 4 teams and 12 drivers in an enduro

Club base includes Endurance; Endurance Plus adds fuel, tyres, driver limits and team phones.
