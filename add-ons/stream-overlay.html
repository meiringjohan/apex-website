Stream Overlay

Home · / year (see Pricing for multi-year)

Race graphics over your track camera: a see-through TV tower while cars race, and a results board between heats and at the end. One transparent HTML page as a browser source.

Who it’s for. Home racers who stream and want a clean tower over the camera.

Features

  • ✓TV tower — laps, average, best and gap paging; place arrows; fastest lap; start lights
  • ✓Results board — heat result, then podium / fastest laps & records / race story
  • ✓Place, size, opacity and logo spin set inside APEX; changes reach the stream live
  • ✓Copy chapters for your recording’s description
  • ✓Works with OBS, Streamlabs, vMix, XSplit, Wirecast

Club includes Stream Overlay in the base. Broadcast Studio adds the directed broadcast tools.
