Endurance Plus

Club · / year (see Pricing for multi-year)

Club enduros with stints and drive-time limits, pit windows, virtual fuel, tyre changes, and each team following the race on a phone.

Who it’s for. Clubs that run serious enduros with the rules sheet in the software.

Features

  • ✓Styles: Fun, Club, or Sanctioned / serious
  • ✓Lane plans: continuous or a club rotation plan (drive / rest / marshal)
  • ✓Virtual fuel tank with warning, power-off or penalty when empty
  • ✓Tyre rule with required changes and windows
  • ✓Driver limits — longest stint, drive time, stints per driver
  • ✓Team phones with PIN; captain requests confirmed by race control
  • ✓Optional penalty protests from the team phone
  • ✓Excel/JSON export
