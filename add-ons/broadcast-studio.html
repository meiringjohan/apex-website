Broadcast Studio

Club · / year (see Pricing for multi-year)

Club’s Stream Overlay base already gives you the tower and results board. Broadcast Studio turns that into a directed broadcast: zones, widgets, Auto / Assist / Manual director, sponsors, OBS scene switching, and a broadcast remote on a phone.

Who it’s for. Clubs that stream club nights and want a directed broadcast.

Features

  • ✓Overlay builder with zones and keep-clear areas over the track
  • ✓Stream director — Auto, Assist or Manual; presets Calm / Standard / Lively / Endurance
  • ✓Moments: lap record, fastest lap, lead change, pit stop, hour recap
  • ✓Sponsors — corner / lower-third slot and centre-board takeover
  • ✓Rehearse a made-up race through the director before race day
  • ✓OBS scene switching and optional replay-buffer saves
  • ✓Broadcast remote on a phone for a Broadcast director login
  • ✓Saved overlays (yours first, copy from the APEX library)
