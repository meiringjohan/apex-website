Event Manager

Club · / year (see Pricing for multi-year)

A day of racing with a programme of stages — qualifying, heats and finals — and drivers imported from a sheet. Vet & start on the morning, then go.

Who it’s for. Clubs that host a one-day open event with imported drivers and a programme.

Features

  • ✓Event wizard: template, phone check-in, date, venue, class, import sheet, rotation, marshalling
  • ✓Import drivers from Excel
  • ✓Phone check-in for the event field
  • ✓Programme — every race with drivers, marshals, status and winner
  • ✓Vet & start — direction for every race of the event
  • ✓Excel/JSON export unlocked
