Championship

Home · / year (see Pricing for multi-year)

Turn your home nights into a season. Championship scores points (or total laps, or average lap) across rounds, drops your worst nights, and keeps clear standings.

Who it’s for. Home tracks that run a season of rounds with friends, not just one-off races.

Features

  • ✓Season of rounds on a chosen race template
  • ✓Scoring schemes: points per round, total laps, or average lap time
  • ✓Dropped rounds so missing one night doesn’t end a season
  • ✓Enrollment and seeding (qualifying on round 1, or a manual seeding sheet)
  • ✓Standings after every round; archive finished seasons
  • ✓Wizard setup with review before you save
  • ✓Works with Auto Pilot and the Race Control you already use

Club gets this module in the Club base, and goes further with Championship Plus.
