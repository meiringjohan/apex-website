What the add-ons add

Home add-ons

Championship — seasons with dropped rounds and seeding.
Endurance — team or single-driver enduros with stints, pits and lane changes.
Stream Overlay — live tower and results overlays for OBS and other tools.
Home Complete — all three together.

Club add-ons

Championship Plus — full seasons, seeding, dropped rounds; works with multi-day meetings.
Endurance Plus — stints, pit windows, lane changes, fuel, tyres and team phones.
Event Manager — one-day events with imported drivers and results.
Event Manager Plus — multi-day meetings with carry-over to the finals.

Club add-ons (cont.)

Screen Studio — up to six live screens on the network.
Broadcast Studio — stream director, custom overlays, phone remote.
API — Excel and JSON exports, plus external integration.
Extra Track — run more than one track on the same computer.

Good to know

Club trial

Covers the Club base only. Add-ons are not part of the 30-day trial.

Home limits

Home allows up to 6 drivers racing at once, and up to 12 in an Endurance race. Club has no driver limit.

Hardware

Club needs a Pi 4 or 5, a PC or a Mac. Pi 3 runs Home only. Mac is Apple Silicon.

Languages

English, Spanish and Italian on screen, phones and voice call-outs. This website is English only.

Licence

Per computer and per track. Key checked offline. Gentle expiry with grace period.

No hardware included

APEX is software. You bring the computer, screen and a supported lap counter.
